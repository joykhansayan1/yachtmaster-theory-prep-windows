Support

Need help with Yachtmaster Theory Prep for Windows? Email dmjks120@gmail.com and we will reply within 2 business days.

Restore purchases

Open the App, go to Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account you used to buy.

Cancel a subscription

Go to account.microsoft.com/services, find Yachtmaster Theory Prep and choose Cancel.

Reset progress

Settings → Reset progress clears answers, streak and mock history on this device. It does not affect your purchase.
