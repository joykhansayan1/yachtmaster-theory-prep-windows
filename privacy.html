Privacy Policy

Yachtmaster Theory Prep for Windows · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yachtmaster Theory Prep for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

Study progress (answers, streak, bookmarks, mock history, topic mastery, preferences, course/exam date) is stored only in the App's local storage on your Windows device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Network use

The App works offline. It connects to the internet only to download app updates and to let the Microsoft Store verify your purchases.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

6. Children

The App is an education study aid for adult recreational and professional sailors. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: dmjks120@gmail.com.

This App is an independent, unofficial study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Royal Yachting Association (RYA) or any other awarding body.
